Our Story

15 Years. 3,200+ Systems. One Standard.

Elite Climate Solutions was founded by Dave Marchetti, a TSSA-licensed gas technician who spent years watching homeowners get vague quotes and substandard repairs. He started Elite in 2009 with two trucks, a commitment to flat-rate pricing, and a 24/7 phone line he answered himself.

Today we run a team of 12 licensed HVAC technicians and service over 3,200 systems per year across Kitchener, Cambridge, Waterloo, and surrounding communities. The flat-rate pricing and 24/7 availability Dave insisted on in year one still apply to every job we take.

We're also one of the region's most active Enbridge rebate processors, having helped Waterloo Region homeowners recover over $1.8M in heat pump and efficiency rebates since the program launched.

How We Operate

Always Available

24/7 emergency line, every day of the year. No answering services, no callbacks the next morning; a licensed tech picks up and dispatches within the hour.

Flat Rate, Always

No hourly billing that spirals during a repair. Every job gets a flat written price before we start. The number on your invoice matches the number you approved. Guaranteed.

Qualified on Every Job

Every technician holds a current TSSA licence. We don't send trainees alone or use unlicensed sub-trades. You get qualified hands on your system, every time.

Rebate Specialists

We're active Enbridge and federal rebate processors. We handle the application, not just the installation, so you get the money back, not just a suggestion to apply.
