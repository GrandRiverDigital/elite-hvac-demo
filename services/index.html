Preventative Care

Annual HVAC Maintenance

Annual tune-ups are the single best investment for your HVAC system. Equipment that receives yearly maintenance lasts 3 to 5 years longer, runs 15–20% more efficiently, and is far less likely to fail during extreme weather. We offer separate fall furnace and spring AC plans, or a combined plan that covers both seasons.

  • Complete safety inspection of heat exchanger, burners, and flue
  • Filter replacement and blower cleaning
  • Refrigerant level check and coil cleaning (AC plan)
  • Thermostat calibration and testing
  • Lubrication of moving components
  • Written report with any recommendations at no obligation

Indoor Air Quality

Air Quality & Ventilation

Modern homes are well-sealed for energy efficiency, but that also means stale, recirculated air. An HRV (heat recovery ventilator) continuously exchanges indoor air with fresh outdoor air while retaining most of the thermal energy. Combined with whole-home humidification and HEPA filtration, you get air quality that genuinely supports health, not just comfort.

  • HRV installation, cleaning, and core replacement
  • Whole-home bypass and flow-through humidifiers
  • HEPA media and electronic air cleaner installation
  • UV germicidal light installation for ductwork
  • Ductwork inspection and sealing
  • Carbon monoxide detector installation and testing
